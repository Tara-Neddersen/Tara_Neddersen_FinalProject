# People

One file per person that matters in your life. The assistant reads these
when it encounters email from them. Filename: `<email-address>.md` —
lowercase, at-sign replaced with underscore.

Example: `knowles_stanford.edu.md` for Prof. Knowles.

## Template

```markdown
# Dr. Jane Knowles

**Relationship:** My PhD advisor.
**How I talk to her:** Respectfully, directly, never hedging on facts but
always honest about constraints. First-name basis in email.
**Currently:** She's PI on two grants I'm on. She cares most about the
brain-extraction paper we're writing. She doesn't micromanage.
**Response time expectation:** Same day for anything short; within 48h for
anything that needs thought.
**What she hates:** Being told "no" without an alternative. Over-apologizing.
**Special context:** She's been patient with me while I'm burnt out. Don't
take advantage of that.
```

You don't need a file for everyone — just the people whose email has a
different shape than the default. For strangers, the assistant falls back
to the `about_me.md` priorities.
